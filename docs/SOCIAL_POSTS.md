## LinkedIn
Spent today at Dwelly's Real-World Agents Hackathon in London building Resolve AI,
an agent for property maintenance.

A tenant says "the boiler's stopped working". Resolve AI works out what's wrong,
checks the property and repair history, picks the right contractor, books the job,
keeps the tenant and landlord informed — and stops to involve a human when it
should (gas, electrics, approvals, conflicting records).
